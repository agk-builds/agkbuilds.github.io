Sheet 03 / 04 · Method

How we work

A drawing comes before the build, and a handover comes after it.

  1. 01Step

    Understand the job

    We learn what the software has to do and how your business works today. Questions come before code.

  2. 02Step

    Draw the plan

    We turn what we learned into a plan: what gets built, in what order, and what finished looks like.

  3. 03Step

    Build it

    We build the software to the plan, one part at a time. What was drawn becomes what runs.

  4. 04Step

    Hand it over

    The finished software is handed over to you, along with what you need to use it.
